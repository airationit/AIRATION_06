# Hirance Trending Content & SEO Title Research Prompt

This prompt is engineered to research and generate high-trending, high-CTR, SEO-optimized article titles for **Hirance.com** (India's next-gen swipe-based job hiring platform). It specifically targets **Jobs, Companies, Startups, and AI** within the **Indian job market**.

---

## 📋 The Master Research Prompt

> **Copy and paste the prompt below into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity:**

```markdown
You are an elite Talent Market Researcher, SEO Content Strategist, and Tech Journalist specialized in the Indian job market, Indian startup ecosystem, and AI recruitment trends.

Your goal is to research and identify the highest-trending, high-search-intent, and viral article titles for the Hirance.com blog (Hirance is a modern, swipe-based, fast job-matching platform in India).

### 🎯 Target Audience
1. **Indian Job Seekers & Tech Talent:** Freshers (Tier-1, 2, 3 college grads), junior to senior software engineers, data analysts, AI/ML engineers, and product managers across Bengaluru, NCR (Delhi/Gurgaon/Noida), Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, and Chennai.
2. **Indian Employers & Founders:** Early-stage startup founders, HR heads, and hiring leads looking to hire verified talent fast without waiting weeks on legacy job portals.

### 🔍 Core Thematic Pillars (Focus: India)
1. **Jobs & Career Mobility in India:**
   - Real salary benchmarks in LPA (e.g., 6 LPA vs 18 LPA, Fresher to Mid-level hikes).
   - Off-campus placements, ATS rejection truths, resume-free/swipe-based hiring.
   - High-demand tech roles and hiring speed in tier-1 vs tier-2 Indian cities.

2. **Companies & Workplace Dynamics in India:**
   - The GCC (Global Capability Center) hiring explosion across Hyderabad and Bengaluru.
   - Product companies vs Service IT giants (TCS, Infosys, Wipro) vs Funded Startups.
   - Return-to-office mandates vs remote/hybrid work policies in Indian tech hubs.

3. **Indian Startup Ecosystem & Hiring:**
   - Early-stage startup hiring culture, ESOPs vs cash components in Indian CTC.
   - Startup hiring freezes vs aggressive recruitment in profitable sectors.
   - What founders look for when hiring the first 10 engineers in India.

4. **AI & Emerging Tech Disruption in India:**
   - How Generative AI is reshaping fresher hiring and entry-level IT jobs in India.
   - Realistic AI engineer career switch roadmaps for Indian developers.
   - How modern AI and smart match scores are replacing keyword-stuffed resumes.

---

### ✍️ Title Construction Rules & Formulas
- **Zero Fluff / Non-AI Sounding:** Avoid generic phrases like "A Comprehensive Guide to...", "The Ultimate Roadmap...", or "Unlocking Your Potential".
- **Humanized & Direct:** Write like an experienced Indian tech lead, hiring manager, or seasoned talent analyst.
- **Numbers & Specificity:** Use real metrics, years (2026), LPA figures, and Indian cities wherever relevant.
- **High Click-Through & Search Volume:** Balance SEO keywords (high search volume on Google India) with strong curiosity or value hooks.

---

### 📊 Required Output Format

For each of the 4 Pillars, provide 5 top-tier trending titles formatted as follows:

#### [Pillar Name]
1. **Title:** [Crisp, high-CTR title]
   - **Target Keyword(s):** [Primary Google India search queries]
   - **Search Intent:** [Informational / Career Decision / Hiring Urgency]
   - **Why It's Trending in India Right Now:** [1-2 sentences on current market relevance]
   - **Key Angle / Hook:** [The unique perspective or data-backed insight the article will cover]

---

### 🚀 Bonus Section:
Provide **5 High-Impact Contrarian / Thought Leadership Titles** that challenge conventional Indian hiring practices (e.g., why 90-day notice periods are dying, why ATS systems fail Indian candidates, or how swipe hiring cuts time-to-hire by 80%).
```

## 📌 Verification Checklist for Generated Titles

Before selecting titles for publication on Hirance:
- [ ] **Is it specifically relevant to India?** (Mentions LPA, Indian hubs, tier cities, CTC breakdown, or Indian workplace norms).
- [ ] **Does it avoid AI cliches?** (No "Delve", "Demystifying", "Comprehensive Guide", "In Today's Fast-Paced World").
- [ ] **Is it crisp and under 70 characters?** (Ensures full display on Google Search SERPs without truncation).
- [ ] **Does it align with Hirance's core brand?** (Speed, smart compatibility scores, verified candidates, zero forms, modern swipe hiring).
